<h1 align="center">Better Connections</h1>
<p align="center"><em>Fences, Walls, and Iron Bars — now connected.</em></p>
<br>
<hr>
<br>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>
Better Connections is a lightweight NeoForge mod that makes fences, walls, and iron bars visually connect to each other. No more gaps or mismatched edges — your builds will look seamless and cohesive.
</p>
<p>
This is a port of <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/betterwalls">BetterWalls</a> by <strong>Lemonnik6484</strong> and <strong>JX_Snack</strong>, originally built for Fabric.
</p>
<br>
<h2>Features</h2>
<h3>Fences</h3>
<p>
Fences connect to walls, other fences, iron bars, and wall signs. No more floating fence posts next to a wall — they join naturally.
</p>
<h3>Iron Bars</h3>
<p>
Iron bars connect to fences, fence gates, and wall signs. Perfect for decorative barriers and mixed-material builds.
</p>
<h3>Walls</h3>
<p>
Walls connect to fences and wall signs. Create hybrid fences and walls that actually touch.
</p>
